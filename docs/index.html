    Common Thread

    Somewhere, someone has the answer to your problem, or is stuck on the same one. This globe draws five kinds of quiet connection from real public records.

      A bright common thread appears where two layers genuinely meet, for example an abandoned project that tackled a problem people are working on again today.

      Each mark carries a small trust note: who published it, its licence, and how fresh it is. A daily check re-reads a sample of records against their originals and hides any source that has gone stale or broken. Everything shown is a real public record with a link. Nothing is invented; where a source is thin or missing, the page says so. Nobody is contacted. Introductions will come later, and only with consent. "Common Thread" is a working name.
